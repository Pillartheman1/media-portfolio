Script, intro

In February 2021, I joined INSTAGRAM! In the year that followed, I leveled up my photography skills and got into the habit of sharing every photo I took with Instagram.

And I won’t lie, it helped me improve a lot!

In addition to shooting nothing but vertical photos for a year, I learned how to organize my feed, optimize my colors, and improve my branding skills. But despite all this progress, it was causing me to lose FUCKING MIND

Instagram was getting in the way of my work, my social life, and even my mental health! That’s why, one year later, I decided enough was enough

And I DELETED INSTAGRAM. And chances are, you should too.
