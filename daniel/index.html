Shot listing

I write a hyper-detailed shot list and a script in Notion for every project, personal and client. Each line is a precise visual description: shot composition and framing, lens choice, camera movement, lighting, and how the shot should sit in the cut.

That written direction is what I shoot from, and what I use afterward to evaluate the footage against a written spec — whether the continuity holds, and whether the pacing still has the temporal coherence I planned on the page.

Example shot list

01Porch, last light

I stand in the doorway, one hand on the frame, looking out at the street. Don't start walking yet.

Eye-level, slightly off-axis, the door as a second frame. Golden hour, sun just under the roofline; warm spill on the left cheek, porch in open shade

MS35mmStatic, locked off on sticksA-roll4s
02Front steps

Feet take the first two steps down and pause on the third.

High angle, looking down the steps. Same last light, no fill; let the shadows go

CU50mmTilt down with the first step, then holdB-roll3s
03Sidewalk under the trees

Walking and talking, eyeline just off-lens — not a straight vlog hold.

Eye-level, beside me, leading room ahead. Dappled late light; watch the flicker through the leaves so it doesn't strobe the cut

MS28mmGimbal, match pace; a gentle push-in over the last lineA-roll8s
04Corner, looking back

I stop and look back at the house. Hold the look longer than is comfortable.

Across the street, house and figure in the same frame. Sky still holding a little color; the house starting to go to silhouette

WS35mmStatic; start on the house, I walk into frame and stopB-roll6s
