Zaidi Jewelers

Recurring content for a premium jewelry brand in Indianapolis.

Edited by Editron

These cuts were edited by Editron, the AI editing system I built, from footage of the same Zaidi Jewelers work.

Overview

Zaidi Jewelers, a premium jewelry brand in Indianapolis, is one of my most regular clients. I make recurring content for the brand, and the videos here are a selection of that work. One is a close look at pendants on a bust. Another puts the pieces on a person: earrings, a necklace, rings. A third follows a ring from a pencil sketch to the finished piece in its box. Another opens on a Zaidi Jewelers box and then shows the jewelry being worn.
