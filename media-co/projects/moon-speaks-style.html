Overview

I made a short vertical video for Moon Speaks Style, Moon's personal brand. Moon also owns Carmel Tailoring; this piece is for the personal brand, not for the shop. It's shot in close: a cigar being cut and lit, a skeleton watch, the texture of a jacket. The piece ends on a Klabunde Media Company card.
