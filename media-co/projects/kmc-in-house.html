Overview

These videos were made for Klabunde Media Co. itself, not for a client.

I make long-form videos where I break films down, sometimes shot by shot: composition, lens, camera movement, lighting, blocking, and pacing, and I try to say clearly why a shot works. That kind of shot-by-shot analysis is a visual evaluation of the footage — describing it precisely, and paying attention to temporal continuity from one cut to the next. It's the same habit I use when I write a shot list, and when I look at the footage after a shoot. The seven long-form videos here are from the Klabunde Media Co. YouTube channel. Some of them go through a film that way; the titles include Fight Club and Tron: Ares. Others apply the same kind of attention to commercials, to how movie craft shows up in other work, and to video editing programs.

The short-form videos are Instagram posts, also made for the company, posted from October 2025 to September 2026.
