The RX Building · CrossFit Murph

An event recap of the CrossFit Murph at The RX Building.

Overview

I made an event recap for The RX Building, a community health center and CrossFit gym, covering their CrossFit Murph. The video is a minute long and stays with the people in the gym: athletes working, a group coming through the doors, close shots of the barbells and the effort on people's faces. It opens and closes on black.
