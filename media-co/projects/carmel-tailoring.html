Overview

Carmel Tailoring, the most prestigious men's clothier and tailor in the Indianapolis area, owned by Moon, is one of my most consistent clients. I make recurring content for the brand, and the videos here are a selection of that work.

Among them are landscape cinematic films. One follows a man in a burgundy suit, from a close look at his watch through an interior with bookshelves and a painting. Another moves with a man in tailored suits: walking through glass doors, sitting in a paneled room, sitting outside. A third stays in the shop — jackets on the rack, a suited mannequin, hanging cloth. Another is about the craft: a fitting with a tape measure, and a card showing stitching samples, including standard stitching and picked stitching.

Short verticals look at the materials and the clothes on a person: fabric labels and swatch books, a man putting on a plaid jacket, and close details of a jacket cuff, with a shot of a man in a suit by a pond.

The interviews include a vertical piece with a menswear designer at Carmel Tailoring; a landscape film in which Kay introduces herself as the owner and founder and talks about the people she gets to dress; and a longer sit-down in the shop with several people.
